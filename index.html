I work on Efficient Deep Learning and Reliable AI, with a focus on model compression, robustness under domain shift, tail-risk control, and efficient vision-language models.

My current research develops Risk-Aware Vetting (RAV), a tail-risk-aware framework for reliable layer collapse under heterogeneous domain shifts. I am also exploring dynamic visual token reduction for large multimodal models such as LLaVA.

Research

My research goal is to design efficient AI systems that remain reliable under real-world variability, distribution shift, and resource constraints.

  • Efficient Deep Learning: model compression, layer collapse, pruning, and scalable architectures.
  • Reliability and Robustness: tail-risk control, domain generalization, and distribution-sensitive evaluation.
  • Vision-Language Models: dynamic visual token reduction for efficient multimodal reasoning.
  • Risk-Aware Learning: risk-sensitive decision rules for trustworthy model adaptation.
