Vishnu Pratap Singh Parihar

PhD Researcher · Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence · IIT Roorkee

I work on Efficient Deep Learning and Reliable AI systems, focusing on robustness under domain shift, uncertainty, and real-world variability. My research aims to bridge the gap between accuracy and reliability in modern machine learning systems.

My work introduces Risk-Aware Vetting (RAV), a tail-risk-aware framework for reliable structural model compression. I also work on efficient vision-language models, including dynamic token adaptation for large multimodal systems such as LLaVA.

Research

My research focuses on building AI systems that are not only efficient but also reliable under distribution shift and real-world constraints.

  • Efficient Deep Learning: Model compression and scalable architectures
  • Reliability & Robustness: Tail-risk control and domain generalization
  • Vision-Language Models: Efficient multimodal learning and token pruning
  • Risk-Aware Learning: Distribution-sensitive optimization and evaluation
