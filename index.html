Vishnu Pratap Singh Parihar

PhD Researcher · Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence · IIT Roorkee

I am a PhD researcher working at the intersection of computer vision, model compression, and reliable deep learning. My research focuses on making visual intelligence systems—CNNs, Vision Transformers (ViTs), and large Vision-Language Models (VLMs)—both efficient and trustworthy under real-world variability and distribution shift.

My current work develops Risk-Aware Vetting (RAV), a tail-risk-aware framework for reliable structural pruning of neural networks under heterogeneous domain shifts, validated across CNNs and ViTs on benchmarks such as PACS and OfficeHome. In parallel, I am designing DynaPruMerge, a decode-aware dynamic visual token reduction method for large multimodal models (LLaVA-7B, LLaVA-13B, LLaVA-NeXT), targeting efficient and context-preserving generative visual reasoning.

I am advised by Prof. Sanjeev Kumar (Head, DS&AI, IIT Roorkee) and Prof. Balasubramanian Raman (Head, CSE, IIT Roorkee). Prior to my PhD, I was a Junior Research Fellow at DRDO's Naval Science & Technological Laboratory (NSTL), Visakhapatnam, where I worked on applied statistical modeling for maritime defense systems.

Research

My overarching goal is to develop AI systems that are simultaneously efficient, reliable, and generalizable—capable of performing under distribution shift and resource constraints, not just on controlled benchmarks. I approach this through the lens of risk-sensitive statistics, optimization, and modern deep learning for visual understanding.

Model Compression & Pruning

Structural pruning, layer collapse, and efficient neural architecture design for CNNs and Vision Transformers. Emphasis on preserving task performance under domain heterogeneity.

Risk-Aware & Reliable Learning

Tail-risk control using Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR), distribution-sensitive evaluation, and risk-sensitive decision rules for trustworthy model adaptation and compression.

Vision-Language Models

Dynamic visual token reduction for LLaVA architectures; decode-aware mechanisms that preserve critical visual evidence during generative multimodal reasoning.

Domain Generalization

Robustness under domain shift, benchmarking on PACS and OfficeHome, and principled frameworks to prevent hidden failures in compressed models across heterogeneous data distributions.
